PRIVACY POLICY

Nap Time Pals is a puzzle game for children, made by Redkeep Studios.
Effective date: 23 November 2026

1. WHO WE ARE
Redkeep Studios is the publisher of Nap Time Pals. If you have any questions
about this policy or the game, contact us at: redkeepstudios@gmail.com

2. THE SHORT VERSION
- The game has no accounts, no chat and no sign-up.
- We do not ask players for names, email addresses, photos or contact details.
- Game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
- The game shows ads from Google AdMob. They are set up for children: they are
  not personalised, and they are limited to content suitable for all ages.
- The ads software, provided by Google, automatically handles some technical
  information (see section 4).

3. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE
The game saves your progress (levels completed, stars earned, pets unlocked)
and your settings (sound, helper marks, chosen pet) in a small file on your
device. This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes
it. You can also erase your progress at any time in the game's Settings screen.

4. ADVERTISING
The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Because the game is made for
children, we tell AdMob to treat all requests as child-directed and as coming
from users under the age of consent. This means:
- ads are not personalised, and are not based on a player's interests or past
  behaviour;
- ads are limited to a maximum content rating of "G" (suitable for general
  audiences);
- the game does not use the Android advertising ID.
To show ads, the game needs an internet connection. The Google ads software
automatically processes technical information, such as the device's IP address,
how the ads are used (for example taps and video views), diagnostic information
about the app, and device identifiers that are not advertising IDs. Google uses
this to deliver ads, measure how they perform, and prevent fraud. We do not
receive this information. You can read how Google handles it here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Pet Nap Puzzle is designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children, and the game does not include features that let
children share information or contact other people. Parents and carers: if you
believe a child has given us personal information, please email us and we will
delete it.

6. OTHER SERVICES
The game does not include analytics, social media features, in-app purchases or
links to outside websites. If this changes, we will update this policy first.

7. YOUR RIGHTS
Because we do not collect personal information about players, we hold no
personal data to access, correct or delete. If you email us, we will use your
email address only to reply to you, and will delete it afterwards.

8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
If we change this policy, we will publish the new version at the same web
address and update the effective date above.
